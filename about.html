OUR STORY

Built, Not Bought. Hustled, Not Handed. Earned, Not Given.

Quality is our quantity — we deliver curb appeal you can see from the street.

At FLA Property Maintenance LLC, we believe a well-kept property says a lot. We founded our business on 100% hard work, honest pricing, and a simple promise to Central Florida property owners: show up on time, do the job right, and treat every yard as if it were our own. While other lawn services rush through properties or skip the small details, we take pride in delivering crisp edges, healthy turf, and spotless walkways on every single visit.

From quick one-time Mow & Go cuts for overgrown yards to scheduled weekly maintenance and complete front yard landscape makeovers, our crew brings relentless energy to every project. We operate 7 days a week, rain or shine, so homeowners and property managers in Lakeland, Winter Haven, and surrounding communities never have to worry about their yard getting out of control.
